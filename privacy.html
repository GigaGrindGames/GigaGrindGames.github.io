KESSARA: Protocol 2099 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026 · App: KESSARA: Protocol 2099 (Android package com.GigaGrindGames.kessara2099, iOS bundle com.GigaGrindGames.kessara2099) · Developer: GigaGrindGames · Contact: gigagrindgames@gmail.com

1. What the game does not collect

The game has no registration or accounts of its own. It does not ask for your name, phone number or e-mail and does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

2. Game progress

Your progress and settings are stored on your device (in the app's private storage). If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also kept in your Google account (Play Games “Saved Games”) so that it can be restored on another device. On Android progress may also be included in your device's Google backup, on iOS in your iCloud device backup, so that it returns after reinstalling. The developer does not receive this data.

3. Advertising — AppLovin MAX and its partners

The game shows ads (rewarded videos you choose to watch, interstitials between game sessions and a banner) through AppLovin MAX and the ad networks it works with (such as Google AdMob / Ad Manager, Mintegral, BIGO Ads, BidMachine, Yandex Ads). To deliver, measure and limit ads, these companies may process your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA — on iOS only with your permission), device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), IP address and approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad settings. You can also reset or turn off the advertising ID in your device settings.

AppLovin privacy policy: applovin.com/privacy · Google: policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never see your payment details; the game receives only a purchase confirmation to deliver the item.

5. Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), your scores, public player name and a short in-game tag (car skin, level, faction) are sent to that service to show leaderboards to other players.

6. Anonymous game statistics

To find out where players get stuck and what to improve, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to the developer: a random installation ID created by the game itself (it is not linked to your Google account, advertising ID or any device identifier, and clearing the app's data creates a new one), app version, game language, which screens and game actions are used and for how long, tutorial progress, player level and cleared cities, and ad and purchase events (an ad was requested, shown or rewarded; a purchase of a product ID was started, completed or cancelled). The statistics contain no name, e-mail, advertising ID, IP address or location. They are stored on Cloudflare servers, used only by the developer to improve the game and never sold or shared; all of it is deleted automatically within 90 days.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all progress stored on the device. Data held by advertising partners is kept according to their policies. To request deletion or ask any question about your data, write to gigagrindgames@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
